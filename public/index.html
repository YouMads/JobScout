A CLEARER WAY TO SEARCH

What does what?

Three parts work together. You decide when to use the AI and when to take the next step.

1AUTOMATIC

Find opportunities

Job feeds supply the listings. Your role, location, experience, and keywords filter them.

2LOCAL AI

Understand your fit

Ask the AI to compare one job with your profile and optional CV. Get a written review.

3YOU CONTROL IT

Track & apply

Save jobs, update stages, and add notes. Open the original listing when you want to apply.

What the AI does

Checking…

When you select Review fit with AI, your local Ollama model compares the job description with the role, skills, experience, and background in your profile. A saved CV is included as extra context.

You get

  • A short summary and suggested next step
  • Potential strengths supported by your information
  • Gaps and questions worth checking
  • Notes about the listed location

You stay in control

  • Reviews run on this PC when requested
  • You can review a job without a CV
  • Suggestions depend on the evidence you provide
  • You confirm requirements and submit applications

Follow-upsDates are checked against today on your PC. Due counts update while this dashboard is open; no email or background notification is sent.
